Put your phone down. Properly.

Kalm locks your phone for the time you choose, from 1 minute to 3 hours. Calls and messages still get through. Everything else waits, and there is no stop button.

How it works

Three steps, and the third one is waiting.

  1. Choose how long

    Drag the ring to anything from 1 minute to 3 hours. Or set a schedule that starts by itself, like an alarm.

  2. Tap Lock

    Any app you have not allowed is covered by a calm lock screen with a countdown.

  3. Wait it out

    Kalm has no stop button. When the timer runs down, your phone is yours again.

What you get

Small on purpose. It does one thing and tries to do it well.

Any length

From 1 minute to 3 hours, one minute at a time. Tap the number to type it instead.

Scheduled locks

Lock at 9 pm every night for an hour without lifting a finger. Choose the days; set up to ten.

Calls still get through

Phone and Messages are always allowed, so people can reach you when it matters.

Only what is essential

You can allow banking and payment apps, authenticator apps and maps. Shopping, social, video, mail and browsers can never be allowed.

Truly Kalm

An optional setting that pauses the timer while you are in an allowed app, so a 2 hour lock really is 2 hours away from your phone.

Your week at a glance

Your streak and the last seven days, in one calm screen. Pick from eight colour themes.

Private by design

  • Kalm has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.
  • Your schedules, allowed apps and history stay on your phone.

Good to know

  • Android 9 or newer.
  • Setup is two switches in Settings. Accessibility lets Kalm see which app is in front, never what is on your screen. Do Not Disturb access lets it hide notifications during a lock. Kalm walks you through both.
